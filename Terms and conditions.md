## 1. The service

Ad Defense is a browser extension that removes unwanted and intrusive content from the pages you
visit: advertisements, pop-ups, banners, trackers and heavy third-party scripts. It also includes optional
tools ("Superpowers") such as the Element Zapper and Anonymous (open the current page in a private
Incognito window), automatic video-ad skipping, and an optional **Page colours** feature
(Beta, off until you switch it on) that shows web pages in dark or another colour you choose and
leaves pages that are already dark as they are. **Every feature is free and works without any
account or sign-in.**

To do this, Ad Defense modifies the pages you visit — hiding ad elements, blocking requests to
advertising hosts, stripping campaign tags from links, skipping video ads, and blocking pop-ups from
advertising networks. Section 5 of the
[Privacy Policy](https://github.com/Ad-Immunity-Ad-Blocker/adimmunity-policy-terms/blob/main/privacy-policy.md)
lists every page modification in full, and Section 6 explains every permission the extension
requests.

**Changing a setting reloads the page you are on.** Turning Ad Defense on or off, or changing any
feature switch, refreshes the tab you are currently viewing so that the change takes effect
immediately — filtering rules only apply to requests made after the change. Only the tab you are
viewing is reloaded, never your other tabs. Page colours and the number on the toolbar icon are
the exceptions: they change without a reload. **A reload discards anything typed into a form on
that page that you have not yet submitted**, so submit or copy unsaved work before toggling a
setting.

**Turbo.** Ad Defense includes an optional speed feature, "Turbo", which blocks page components that
are not advertising — embedded social, video and comment widgets, live-chat widgets, A/B-testing and
tag-manager scripts, and web fonts — and can ask sites for lighter pages using the standard
`Save-Data` request header. **Turbo is switched off unless you turn it on.** The first time you switch
it on, Ad Defense enables four of its five categories for you — deferred embeds, chat widgets,
A/B-testing scripts and the `Save-Data` header — so that the switch has a visible effect; **web-font
blocking is never enabled for you.** Every category can be changed at any time under Settings →
Options, your choices are kept, and this initial set-up happens only once. Each option states its
tradeoff. Blocking these
components will change how some websites look and behave: support chat may be unavailable, sites
running a visual experiment will show their default version, and blocking web fonts changes the
typography of every page, including a site's own fonts. Deferred embeds are replaced with a
click-to-load placeholder, or left to the provider's own visible fallback where one exists. Turbo
does not block payment providers, sign-in providers or CAPTCHA services.

**Cookie-consent banners.** Ad Defense can dismiss cookie-consent banners for you. This is on by
default and can be switched off at any time under **Settings → Options** in the extension. When it
runs, it clicks a recognised consent banner's **"Reject"/"Decline"** button, or, where no such button
exists, its **close/dismiss** button. **Ad Defense never clicks "Accept" or "Agree" and never gives
consent to any site on your behalf.** If a banner can be neither rejected nor closed, Ad Defense
leaves it alone so that you can respond to it yourself. Because refusing consent is the default
position under EU and UK law until you choose otherwise, dismissing a banner this way does not
create a consent record for you — but you remain responsible for reviewing any consent, terms, or
subscription choice a site presents to you.

**Requirements.** Ad Defense requires Google Chrome version 111 or later, or a Chromium-based
browser of equivalent version. It is not supported on earlier versions.
